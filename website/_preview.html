01 — Highlights

Built for people who listen properly.

Every detail of Inzx exists for one reason: to make a song feel closer. Here's what that looks like.

Lyrics engine

Karaoke lyrics, synced to the syllable

BetterLyrics TTML drives true word-level highlighting. When a provider falls short, Inzx silently walks a chain of 15+ sources — LRCLib, Genius, KuGou, Musixmatch, Megalobiz, YouTube captions, embedded tags and local files.

  • Line & word offset tuning
  • Instrumental gaps
  • Background vocals
Audio

Studio-quality sound

A native InnerTube engine streams YouTube Music directly, with an optional JioSaavn source for DES-decrypted 320 kbps audio. Gapless playback, prefetching and crossfade keep the silence out.

Theming

Colors from the cover art

An isolate-based palette extractor pulls colors from album art in effectively 0 ms, cached for next time. Material 3 with 10+ accents and a full custom colour wheel.

AI

Translate & transliterate live

Bring your own key for OpenRouter, DeepL or Gemini and read any song in your language — or in your own script — while it plays.

10 — FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is Inzx free?

Yes — completely. Inzx is released under the MIT license with no paid tier, no ads and no subscription. If you'd like to support it, there's a sponsor link, but nothing is locked behind it.

Do I need a YouTube Music account?

No. Search, playback, lyrics and local files all work signed out. Signing in only adds your own library — synced playlists, likes, albums and artists.

Is it on the Play Store?

No. Inzx is only distributed through GitHub Releases and Obtainium. Any other listing claiming to be official is not ours — please avoid unofficial or modified builds for your own safety.

How does the 320 kbps audio work?

Alongside the native InnerTube engine, Inzx can optionally match a track to JioSaavn and stream its higher-bitrate source (DES-decrypted stream URLs), up to 320 kbps. It's a setting — the YouTube Music source stays available.

Which AI providers can translate lyrics?

OpenRouter, DeepL and Gemini. You supply your own API key, which is stored in encrypted secure storage on your device. Nothing is routed through our servers — there aren't any.

What are Jams?

Real-time listening sessions. A host starts a Jam, friends join, and everyone's playback stays locked to the same position through Supabase Realtime with continuous drift correction. The queue is shared and collaboratively editable.

Does Inzx track me?

No analytics, no telemetry, no third-party tracking SDKs. The app is offline-first and credentials live in encrypted storage. Network requests go to the music services you're actually using.

Is there an iOS or desktop version?

Not yet. Android is the only supported platform today. Windows, macOS and Linux targets are on the roadmap.

How do updates work?

Smaller changes arrive automatically as over-the-air patches via Shorebird code push — no reinstall. Larger releases ship as APKs on GitHub, and Obtainium will pick them up for you.

Can I help?

Please do. Bug reports, feature ideas, translations and pull requests all help. Fork off main, keep flutter analyze clean, and open a PR describing what and why. For larger features, open an issue first so we can align on the approach.
