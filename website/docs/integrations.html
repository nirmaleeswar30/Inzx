Around the app

Integrations & Ecosystem

Discord Rich Presence (RPC)

Broadcast your current listening status directly to Discord:

  • Live Profile Status: Shows "Listening to Inzx" on your Discord profile card.
  • Rich Metadata: Broadcasts current song title, performing artist, album name, and cover artwork thumbnail.
  • Live Timestamps: Shows elapsed track duration and remaining time in real time.
  • Setup: Head to Settings → Discord and log in with your Discord account to authorize Rich Presence updates.

Real-Time Jams

Listen together with friends in perfect synchronization:

  • Sub-Second Sync: Powered by Supabase Realtime WebSockets with continuous clock drift compensation.
  • Collaborative Queues: Anyone in the Jam can propose tracks, reorder upcoming songs, or vote on what plays next.
  • Host Permissions: Host can assign DJ privileges or enforce host-only playback controls to avoid tug-of-war.
  • Instant Join: Friends can join via a 6-digit room code or a direct deep link shared over chat.

Android Auto

Inzx features built-in automotive integration through Android Auto:

  • Distraction-Free UI: High-contrast interface with large, generous touch targets for safe interaction while driving.
  • Steering-Wheel Media Keys: Full integration with steering-wheel seek, skip, and volume buttons via standard Android MediaSession.
  • Car Dashboard Browsing: Browse your Liked Songs, playlists, and cached offline music directly on the vehicle's infotainment screen.

Backup & Restore

Export your app configuration and data to a portable file:

  • Export Data: Settings → Backup & restore bundles your settings, local custom playlists, search history, and listening statistics into an encrypted JSON backup.
  • Restore Anywhere: Easily import your backup when setting up a new phone or migrating builds with zero data loss.
