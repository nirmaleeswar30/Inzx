Sharing links

Share links are short, and show the artwork and title when you paste them into WhatsApp, Discord, Telegram and other apps.

Opening a link

With Inzx installed, a link opens straight in the app. Without it, you'll see a preview page with a button to get Inzx.

Share plain YouTube Music links instead

Settings → App info → Share YouTube Music links makes Inzx share regular music.youtube.com links, for friends who don't use Inzx.
