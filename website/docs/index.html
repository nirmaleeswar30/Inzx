Inzx Docs

Everything Inzx can do, and how to use it

Inzx is a free, open-source YouTube Music client for Android: word-synced lyrics, AI lyric translation, up to 320 kbps audio, dynamic theming and Jams to listen together. These pages explain how to set it up and get the most out of it.

Note

Inzx is not affiliated with YouTube, Google or JioSaavn. It streams from their public services. Official downloads are only on GitHub and this site.

Features at a glance

LyricsWord-by-word karaoke, line sync, AI translation and timing offset. Audio & quality320 kbps sources, crossfade, caching and smart output routing. JamsListen in sync with friends, with shared controls. Sharing linksShort links with artwork previews that open straight in the app. DownloadsSave songs for offline and play your local files. WidgetsThree home screen widgets (2×2, 4×1, 4×4) for instant launcher control.

Need help?

Check Troubleshooting & FAQ first. If your problem isn't there, open an issue on GitHub with your Android version, the Inzx version (Settings → App info) and what you were playing. Settings → Logs lets you export a log to attach.

Something wrong or missing? Open an issue on GitHub.
