Troubleshooting & FAQ

A different song or version plays

That's usually the JioSaavn high quality source matching the wrong track. Turn it off in Settings → Audio → High quality sources. See Audio & quality.

My plays don't show in YouTube Music history

Make sure you're signed in and that history isn't paused in your YouTube Music settings. Local files aren't added to history.

A song won't play

Try again after a few seconds; streams are re-resolved automatically. If it keeps failing, clear the streaming cache in Settings and send the log from Settings → Logs with your issue.

Lyrics are missing or out of sync

Open the lyrics menu (⋯) to pick another provider or adjust the offset. See Lyrics.

Is Inzx free?

Yes. Inzx is open source under the GPL-3.0 license, with no ads and no paid tier.
