Now playing

Tap the mini player to open Now playing. Pick a layout in Settings → Appearance → Now playing style.

Layouts

  • Default: the classic YouTube Music style.
  • Cinematic: full-bleed artwork across the top half, with a like button and menu next to the title.
  • Ripple: wavy artwork with a circular seek ring.
  • OG: the original Inzx layout.

Controls

The control row is the same in every layout: Lyrics on the left, then previous, play/pause, next, and Up Next on the right. The output device (phone speaker, Bluetooth and so on) sits at the very bottom; tap it to switch.

Shuffle and repeat live at the top of the Up Next sheet.

Lyrics, Up Next and Related

  • Lyrics opens the lyrics sheet. You can also swipe up from the left side of the screen.
  • Up Next opens your queue. Swipe across inside it to see Related: similar songs, albums and artists.

Gestures

  • Swipe the artwork left or right to skip.
  • Double-tap the artwork to like.
  • Swipe down to close Now playing.
