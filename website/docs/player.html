Now Playing & Interface

Tap the mini-player or swipe up from the navigation bar to enter the Now Playing console. Personalize the layout in Settings → Appearance → Now Playing style.

Now Playing Styles

  • Default: A modern, high-contrast rendition of the familiar YouTube Music interface with clean rounded album art and spacious controls.
  • Cinematic: Full-bleed widescreen artwork spanning the upper half with atmospheric backdrops, gradient fades, and quick-action like and playlist buttons.
  • Ripple: Dynamic wavy organic contours around the album art coupled with an elegant circular seek ring for one-thumb scrubbing.
  • OG: The signature vintage Inzx aesthetic featuring centered card art, clean borders, and minimalist typography.

Progress Bar & Visualizers

Choose how track playback is visualized in Settings → Appearance → Progress style:

  • Linear: Sleek, standard scrub bar with responsive drag handles and hover timestamps.
  • Waveform: Pre-rendered audio waveform reflecting track dynamics and volume variations.
  • Live Spectrum: Real-time responsive graphic equalizer bouncing with live frequency spectrum analysis.

Liquid Glass Navigation

Inzx features a floating, frosted liquid-glass bottom bar:

  • Mini-Player Vinyl Art: Displays spinning micro album art when playing, pausing when playback stops.
  • Lyric Preview Snippet: Displays the current live sung lyric directly above the navigation bar before opening full-screen lyrics.
  • Adaptive Theming: The glass surface extracts dynamic accent pigments from the active album cover with 0 ms isolate execution.

Controls & Audio Routing

The control row gives you instant access: Lyrics on the far left, previous, play/pause, next, and Up Next (Queue) on the far right. The active audio output device (phone speaker, Bluetooth LDAC/A2DP headset, USB DAC) is shown at the bottom; tap it to open Android's audio switcher.

Gestures

  • Swipe Left / Right: Swipe across the album artwork to skip to the next or previous track.
  • Double-Tap Artwork: Instantly adds the track to your Liked Songs library.
  • Swipe Down: Dismisses the Now Playing screen back to your active tab.
  • Swipe Up: Reveals the full synchronized lyrics sheet.
