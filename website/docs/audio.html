Audio & quality

Streaming quality

Settings → Audio → Streaming quality. Auto adapts to your connection; Max always picks the best available stream.

Lossless add-ons

Inzx can play lossless FLAC from a community add-on when the add-on has a matching copy of the track. Playback can start at normal quality and upgrade once the lossless stream is ready.

  1. Get an add-on endpoint or manifest URL from the provider. Follow the provider’s setup instructions if it gives you a personal URL.
  2. Open Settings → Audio. Under High Quality Sources, choose Add on the Lossless addon or Lossless sources card.
  3. Paste the URL, select Add addon, and make sure the source is enabled.
  4. Set Streaming quality to Auto or Max. Look for Lossless or Hi-Res Lossless during playback to confirm that Inzx found a lossless stream.

Audio on iOS

YouTube streams use AAC. Lossless add-ons can play FLAC/ALAC files and compatible DASH streams, which Inzx bridges to a fragmented-MP4 stream for the native player. Opus/Vorbis add-on streams are skipped.

The native iOS audio effects stage supports EQ, AutoEQ calibration, and loudness processing. With a USB DAC, Inzx asks iOS to use the track’s sample rate. Signal path shows the requested and reported rates. iOS manages the final output, and matching sample rates alone cannot verify bit-perfect playback.

Dolby Atmos on Android, iPhone and iPad

Prefer Dolby Atmos mixes is off by default. Enable it under Settings → Audio when your audio output supports Atmos, and use an add-on that provides an Atmos mix.

When the output lacks Atmos support, Inzx asks the add-on for stereo. The Atmos mix badge appears when Inzx plays an Atmos stream.

DJ Automix

Automix analyses beats and transition cues to blend tracks. On iOS, transitions use live blends, including pairs that would use loop-mix on other platforms. The progress bar pulses while a live blend is active.

High quality sources (JioSaavn)

With this on, Inzx looks for the same song on JioSaavn and, when it finds a match, plays its 320 kbps stream instead of YouTube's.

Heads up

Matching is done by title and artist, so once in a while JioSaavn may load a different song or version. If that happens, turn the source off in Settings → Audio. More high quality sources are coming soon.

Crossfade

Crossfade blends the end of one song into the next. It's on by default at 8 seconds; choose Off, 5, 8, 10 or 12 seconds in Settings → Audio.

Streaming cache

Inzx caches what you play and pre-caches what's next so skipping is instant. You can limit the cache size or restrict pre-caching to Wi-Fi.

Smart audio routing

The device name at the bottom of Now playing shows where audio is going. Tap it to switch outputs.
