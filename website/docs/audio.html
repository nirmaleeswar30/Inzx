Audio & quality

Streaming quality

Settings → Audio → Streaming quality. Auto adapts to your connection; Max always picks the best available stream.

High quality sources (JioSaavn)

With this on, Inzx looks for the same song on JioSaavn and, when it finds a match, plays its 320 kbps stream instead of YouTube's.

Heads up

Matching is done by title and artist, so once in a while JioSaavn may load a different song or version. If that happens, turn the source off in Settings → Audio. More high quality sources are coming soon.

Crossfade

Crossfade blends the end of one song into the next. It's on by default at 8 seconds; choose Off, 5, 8, 10 or 12 seconds in Settings → Audio.

Streaming cache

Inzx caches what you play and pre-caches what's next so skipping is instant. You can limit the cache size or restrict pre-caching to Wi-Fi.

Smart audio routing

The device name at the bottom of Now playing shows where audio is going. Tap it to switch outputs.
